## Capabilities

- **Training** learns policies and writes checkpoints and TensorBoard telemetry.
- **Evaluation** measures a trained policy in finite, repeatable benchmark runs
  and produces comparison-ready TensorBoard evidence.
- **Inference** runs an explicitly selected trained policy for active-use
  scenarios without TensorBoard logging.
- **Comparison packs** combine snapshots of compatible training and evaluation
  evidence, including runs from different models.

Model projects provide environment- and framework-specific behavior through
plugin boundaries. The bundled traffic experiments use Ray RLlib or
Stable-Baselines3 with in-process SUMO/libsumo simulation, while the runtime
contracts remain suitable for non-SUMO domains.
